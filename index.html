    Isometric Šilalė

    A pixel-art map of Šilalė, a Samogitian town of about 5,000 people on the Lokysta river in western Lithuania.

    Every house, road, field and pond comes from OpenStreetMap. Your browser paints each tile from that data: 3,362 buildings with roofs inferred from their footprints, both churches with their towers, and thousands of trees scattered through forests, yards and field edges. Building heights are estimates; OpenStreetMap rarely records them here.

    The idea comes from Justin Chavez’s Isometric SF, which used AI models to paint San Francisco.
